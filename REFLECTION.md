# Reflection — SEO Audit Dashboard

## What was hardest, and why?

Well, during the project I felt lost at some points. My inspiration for why I started somehow went blank, which is part of why it took me so long. Then when I actually started, I had so many questions — where would I get a domain to deploy, or which providers gave a real free API service and not just a trial. After much thinking and consideration, I used Vercel and the Gemini API. Even then, some things were still lagging behind what I expected. I used Claude throughout the project the way we were taught to in the AI courses, working through problems step by step rather than having it just hand me the answer.

## What would I do differently next time?

Next time, I'm considering using Claude Cowork instead, since a more hands-off, multi-step workflow might make the AI engineering side of the project feel less tedious and more enjoyable to work through.

## One thing I learned that surprised me

One thing that genuinely surprised me was how fast AI models get retired. I built the AI integration using `gemini-2.5-flash`, and within the same project it was already closed off to new users in favor of a newer model. I hadn't expected model names to be that short-lived, and it taught me to treat the model name as something that should be configurable, not hardcoded.
